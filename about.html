About the project

About and Contact

Sattva Intelligence is a long-term reading project concerned with texts, arguments, and the public record of interpretation.

Lok Pokhrel

Lok Pokhrel is a Visiting Research Associate in the Department of Religion at Rutgers University and a communication scholar based in Vancouver.

The project approaches classical Indic texts through philosophy, ethics, psychology, and communication. Sources are named, interpretive claims are distinguished from translations, and substantive errors are corrected publicly.

The main series reads the Bhagavata Purana chapter by chapter. A second strand examines the Bhagavad Gita, the Yoga Sutras, the Nyaya Sutras, and questions in comparative Indic philosophy as they relate to modern problems.
