Bhagavata Purana · Episode 1 · Canto 1, Chapters 16–19

Seven Days to Live

A King, a Curse, and the Question of What Is Worth Knowing

Watch Episode 1 on YouTube

Central Question What is worth knowing when time is limited?

Episode Overview

A king is told that he will die in seven days. He does not search for a cure or attempt to preserve his position. He relinquishes his kingdom, sits beside the river, and turns toward a question that will shape the rest of the Bhagavata Purana: what should a person hear, remember, and understand when very little time remains?

This opening episode follows the dialogue between Dharma and the Earth, King Parikshit's confrontation with Kali, the incident that leads to the curse, and the king's response when he learns that his remaining life will be measured in days. The episode ends with the arrival of Shuka and the beginning of the inquiry within which the larger narrative unfolds.

  • The Bhagavata's opening orientation toward questions of origin, reality, and ultimate knowledge

  • The places where King Parikshit permits Kali to remain
  • The forest encounter that leads to the curse
  • The young brahmana's curse and his father's judgment that the response has gone too far
  • Viveka and vairagya, with clarification from the Yoga Sutras
  • Parikshit's response after learning that he has seven days to live
  • The arrival of Shuka and the question that opens the larger teaching

Question for Reflection and Discussion

If you knew your time was short, what is the one thing you would most want to understand before the end? Not what would you want to do. What would you want to understand?
